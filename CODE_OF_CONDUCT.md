# Code of Conduct

## Scope

This code applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, release coordination,
security-report interactions, and project-related public or private communication. It applies to
contributors, maintainers, representatives, and guests whenever they act on behalf of Agent-Safe
Pipeline or Decionis.

## Expected conduct

- Discuss the work and its evidence, not a person's identity or motives.
- Give specific, actionable feedback and allow reasonable time for a response.
- Respect privacy, coordinated-disclosure boundaries, and requests for anonymity.
- Disclose conflicts of interest and recuse from decisions when impartiality is doubtful.
- Help create an environment where people can disagree without intimidation or retaliation.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, sustained disruption, deliberate
misrepresentation, retaliation, and publishing another person's private or security-sensitive
information are not accepted. A position of authority, employment, or technical ownership does not
create an exception.

## Reporting and escalation

For a non-sensitive concern, contact a maintainer or open a repository issue that contains only the
minimum public context. Do not publish private incident details.

For a sensitive concern, email `security@decionis.com` with the subject `Code of Conduct - private`.
This mailbox is the private intake path; a conduct report is handled separately from a vulnerability
report. If the report concerns the project lead or the mailbox operator, contact the alternate
maintainer, [@yunaremaia](https://github.com/yunaremaia), through a contact method on their GitHub
profile and ask to establish a private channel before sharing details.

The recipient must recuse anyone named in the report. An uninvolved maintainer records the minimum
facts needed, confirms receipt, assesses immediate safety or access risks, and assigns a handler.
If no project maintainer is independent, the matter is escalated to an uninvolved Decionis officer
or the relevant hosting platform. Imminent threats may be reported to the appropriate emergency or
legal authority.

## Enforcement and appeals

Responses are proportional to the conduct and may include a private correction, warning, temporary
participation restriction, removal of project access, or a platform report. Good-faith reporters
and witnesses must not be retaliated against. Information is shared only with people needed to
assess and resolve the report, subject to legal and safety obligations.

A person affected by an enforcement decision may request review by an uninvolved maintainer. The
reviewer documents whether the decision is upheld, changed, or returned for further fact-finding.
Project roles and the authority to change access are defined in [GOVERNANCE.md](./GOVERNANCE.md).
